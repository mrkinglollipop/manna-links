Manna

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

The short version. Manna has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Most of what Manna stores never leaves your device. The parts that do are the social parts — your display name, your profile photo, and your daily results — and those are visible to other Manna players by design, because that is what the friends and challenge features are for.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Manna iOS app, published by King Lollipop. It describes what the app stores, where it goes, and what control you have over it.

What stays on your device

Unless you use the social features, Manna is a single-player game and its data stays with you:

  • Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, and past results.
  • Your current board, hint state, and Library pack progress.
  • App preferences, such as whether you have enabled the daily reminder.
  • Your block list.

Some of this is also copied into your own private iCloud storage so your progress follows you between your devices. That copy belongs to your Apple Account. It is handled by Apple, and we cannot read it.

What other players can see

Manna's friends, challenge, and standings features work through Apple's CloudKit. To make them work, the following is stored in the app's shared CloudKit database, where other people using Manna can see it:

  • Your display name and, if you set one, your profile photo and favourite verse.
  • Your daily result — whether you solved the puzzle, how many mistakes you made, and whether you used a hint. Not the answers you guessed.
  • Challenges, rivalry groups, and scores you take part in, along with who else is in them.
  • Friend requests, friendships, invites, and nudges between you and other players.
  • Reports you submit about another player's profile photo, so they can be reviewed.

Please treat your display name and photo as public within the app. Do not put anything in them you would not want another player to see.

How you are identified

Manna does not ask you to create an account, and it never sees your email address, phone number, or Apple Account password. Apple gives the app an anonymous, app-specific identifier for your iCloud account, and that is what links your results to you. It cannot be used to identify you outside Manna.

Contacts

When you invite someone to a challenge, you can pick a person from your contacts so the invite is addressed by name. Apple's contact picker runs outside the app: Manna never gets access to your contacts list, and it receives only the single name you chose. That name is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud storage, purely as a label for the invite. No phone numbers or email addresses are read or stored.

Photos

If you set a profile photo, you choose it through Apple's photo picker, which hands the app only that one image. Manna does not get access to your photo library.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder or allow challenge notifications, Manna uses Apple's notification service to deliver them. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

What Manna does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no behavioural tracking.
  • No third-party services beyond Apple's own iCloud, CloudKit, and notification infrastructure.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with data brokers or marketers.
  • No location data, no health data, no browsing history, no contact list.

Your choices

  • Hide your results. In the app, open your profile and turn on "Hide my results from friends." This stops publishing new results and deletes every result you have already shared.
  • Change or remove your profile. You can edit your display name and verse, or remove your photo, at any time from your profile.
  • Block and report. You can block another player, which removes them as a friend and stops their requests and nudges, and you can report a profile photo for review.
  • Leave a challenge or rivalry to stop contributing scores to it.
  • Delete everything. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To have your shared data removed from the CloudKit database as well, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

How long data is kept

Daily results, challenges, and rivalry records are kept while they are in use by you or the people you play with, and are removed when you delete them, hide your results, or ask us to remove them. Data held in your own private iCloud storage is kept until you delete the app or remove it from your Apple Account.

Children

Manna is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because display names and profile photos are visible to other players, we ask that a parent or guardian supervise any younger player who uses the social features. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data in transit and at rest is handled by Apple's iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure and protected by Apple's security measures. No system is perfectly secure, so please be thoughtful about what you put in your display name, verse, and photo.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your data: support@kinglollipop.com.
