Support

My progress didn't come back after reinstalling

Progress syncs through your iCloud account. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud in iOS Settings, then open Manna and give it a moment — sync can take a minute on a fresh install.

A challenge or friend link opened the App Store instead of the app

Delete and reinstall Manna, then tap the link again — iOS refreshes its link handling when an app is installed. Links also work by tapping them after the app is installed, not by typing them in.

I found a mistake in a puzzle

Every answer is checked against scripture, but if you believe something is wrong, email us the puzzle date or pack name and the category — we take accuracy seriously and will review it against the text.

How do I stop sharing my results?

In the You tab, tap your profile at the top, then Edit profile, and turn on “Hide my results from friends.” This also deletes results the app previously shared — if you’re offline, the app says so and finishes the deletion next time you’re online. You can leave any challenge, group, or friendship from its own screen.

Something else?

Email support@kinglollipop.com and include what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots help.
